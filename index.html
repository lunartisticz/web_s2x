S2X

An FNF engine based on Psych 0.6.3, for optimization, quality of life, and more.

Info

S2X (Source 2 EXTREME) is an Friday Night Funkin' engine, designed around optimization and more.

The capabilities are: Loading FLAC, AIFF, and WAV, not just OGG, GREAT optimization, custom menus, Scripting support, and more

Small warning: At the time of writing this, it's not out yet. It's still got a lot of work to do.
